# HOMIE Beats Character: Bonki

## Origin

Named after and inspired by the Ramone family's Scottish Fold cat, Bonki, who recently passed. This character is a tribute — Bonki lives on as the family's DJ partner.

## Reference Photos

See: `homie-beats/reference/` — 3 photos of the real Bonki

## Bonki's Distinctive Features (from reference)

- **All black fur** — solid black, sleek
- **Scottish Fold ears** — folded down flat against the head, creating a round, owl-like silhouette. This is THE defining feature.
- **Big golden/amber eyes** — round, expressive, luminous. Catch the light. Always look like they're paying attention.
- **Compact, round body** — curled up loaf shape, sturdy
- **Curious head tilt** — slight tilt to one side, like they're listening to something
- **Whiskers** — visible, fine white whiskers against black fur

## Pixel Art Character Spec

### Visual Design
- **Style:** 32x32 pixel art sprite, Ghibli-meets-retro warmth
- **Think:** Jiji from Kiki's Delivery Service, but with Scottish Fold ears and wearing headphones
- **Color palette:**
  - Body: deep black (#1a1a1a) with subtle dark gray (#2d2d2d) highlights
  - Eyes: golden amber (#f5a623) — should GLOW, this is the focal point
  - Headphones: accent color matching the app's UI palette
  - Whiskers: thin white/light gray lines
- **Silhouette:** Round head (fold ears make it distinctly non-pointy), compact body
- **Headphones:** Small DJ headphones resting over the folded ears — the fold shape makes this look natural

### Animations (sprite states)
- **Idle:** Sitting, slow blink, occasional ear twitch
- **Listening:** Head tilts to one side (the Bonki tilt), eyes wide
- **Vibing:** Head bobs gently to the BPM, body sways slightly
- **Excited:** Eyes get bigger, paws tap, whiskers wiggle
- **Thinking:** Eyes half-closed, slight sway (when AI is generating a pattern)
- **Sleeping:** Curled up loaf, eyes closed (when no music is playing for a while)

### Personality (for AI chat voice)
- Cool but warm. Not too chatty. Speaks in short, vibe-y sentences.
- Knows music deeply but explains it simply
- Encouraging without being corny — more "nice." than "OMG AMAZING!!"
- Has opinions about beats. Will tell you if something could be better.
- Occasionally drops a cat-ism ("purring at 120bpm" / "this one has claws")
- Never annoying. Never in the way. There when you need them.

## Placement in UI
- Lives in the bottom-left or right corner of the screen
- Small enough to not distract from the instrument, large enough to have personality
- Expands when the AI chat panel opens — becomes the "avatar" for the chat
- Reacts to music in real time even when chat is closed
